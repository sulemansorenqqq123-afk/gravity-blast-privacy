Privacy Policy for Gravity Blast

Last Updated: November 6, 2026 • Package: com.gravityblaststudio.gravityblast

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravity Blast ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), an Android mobile application developed by Hope x Craft, handles your information.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Gravity Blast is designed with data minimization as a core principle: all game statistics, levels, progress, and settings remain stored strictly on your local device. We do not operate user accounts, cloud logins, or remote game servers.

The application integrates third-party advertising services provided by Google to support ongoing game development. This document details what is stored on your device, what data is processed by third-party advertising SDKs, your privacy rights, and how you can exercise control over your information.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Gravity Blast uses Android's Jetpack Preferences DataStore to save your game state locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any developer-controlled server.

Specifically, local storage stores:

  1. Game Settings:
    • Sound effects toggle (sound_enabled).
    • Haptic vibration toggle (haptics_enabled).
    • Hint line toggle (hint_enabled).
    • Color-blind shape cue toggle (shapes_enabled).
    • Selected ball skins for Player 1 and Player 2 (p1_skin, p2_skin).
    • AI bot difficulty selection (difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard).
    • Selected game mode (mode: Bot vs Duo).
    • First-launch tutorial completion flag (coach_seen).
  2. Progression and Unlocks:
    • Total experience points (xp).
    • Player level (calculated from XP curve).
    • Unlocked ball themes (Tiger, Leopard, Koi, Fern).
  3. Gameplay Statistics:
    • Match history and win/loss records (bot_played, bot_wins, bot_losses).
    • Duo two-player local match records (duo_played, duo_p1_wins, duo_p2_wins).
    • Consecutive win streaks and personal best win streak (bot_streak, bot_best).
    • Best match score and highest blast combo recorded (best_match, best_combo).
    • Total rounds played and cumulative points scored.
    • Dead board counter (when no 3-in-a-row can be formed).
  4. Daily Challenge Progress:
    • Current daily challenge puzzle date (daily_date).
    • Daily challenge solved timestamp and status (daily_solved_date, daily_in_progress).
    • Daily challenge completion streak and best streak record (daily_streak, daily_best).
  5. Match Recovery Information:
    • In-progress board state in temporary JSON format (saved_match_json), allowing you to resume if the app is closed mid-match. This is cleared immediately upon match completion.
  6. Advertising Frequency Counter:
    • Counter of completed standard matches (matches_since_last_ad), used strictly on-device to enforce the 1-match frequency cap before offering an interstitial ad opportunity.

3. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

Gravity Blast does not collect personal identity information directly. However, to display advertisements, the app integrates two official Google software development kits (SDKs):

  • Google Mobile Ads (GMA) Next-Gen SDK (version 1.6.0)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (version 4.0.0)

A. Advertising Format & Placement Rules

  • Interstitials Only: Gravity Blast displays full-screen interstitial ads only. The game does not contain banner ads, audio ads during gameplay, or rewarded video paywalls.
  • Natural Transitions Only: Ads are shown exclusively at natural pauses when a user completes a match and voluntarily taps the NEW MATCH or PLAY AGAIN button.
  • Never During Gameplay: Ads are never displayed during active board play, ball-drop physics animations, line blast shockwaves, victory celebrations, tab navigation, in-game resets, or during Daily Challenges.
  • Non-Blocking Fallback: If an ad is unavailable or fails to load, the game starts the next match immediately without delaying or blocking the player.

B. Information Processed by Google Mobile Ads

Depending on your device settings, operating system version, and consent choices, Google's advertising services may automatically collect and process:

  • Online Identifiers: Google Play Services Advertising ID (GAID), vendor identifiers, or device identifiers.
  • Network Information & IP Address: IP address, which may be used by Google to estimate general geographic location (e.g., country or city level) for regional compliance and ad serving.
  • Device & System Technical Data: Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, screen size, language settings, and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, click events, viewability metrics, ad load times, and diagnostic crash logs.

C. Purposes of Third-Party Processing

Google processes this information to:

  • Deliver interstitial advertisements according to applicable regulations.
  • Enforce frequency capping and viewability verification.
  • Prevent fraudulent traffic, bots, and invalid clicks.
  • Comply with applicable regional privacy frameworks (such as GDPR, UK GDPR, and US State privacy laws).

Google acts as an independent data controller for its advertising technologies. To review Google's full privacy policies and learn how Google processes data, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

4. User Consent and In-App Privacy Controls

A. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

On first launch, or when required by applicable regional laws (such as in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland), Gravity Blast uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a regional consent form. This form informs you about data processing and allows you to make individualized consent choices regarding advertising technologies.

B. In-App Privacy & Ad Preferences Option

You have ongoing control over your consent choices at all times:

  1. Open Gravity Blast.
  2. Navigate to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap PRIVACY & AD PREFERENCES.
  4. The Google UMP privacy management dialog will open directly, allowing you to review or modify your consent, opt out of personalized advertising, or withdraw consent previously granted.

C. System-Level Device Controls

You can also control advertising identifiers directly in your Android device settings:

  • Android Settings > Google > Ads: You can reset your Advertising ID or delete your Advertising ID entirely to prevent cross-app interest-based advertising tracking.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

To be completely clear about our boundaries, Gravity Blast:

  • Does NOT require an account: There is no registration, email sign-up, phone verification, or password.
  • Does NOT collect contact information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • Does NOT collect precise location: The game does not request or use ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION GPS permissions.
  • Does NOT access device media or files: We do not read your photo gallery, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • Does NOT run proprietary telemetry or tracking: We do not operate third-party analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK).
  • Does NOT sell personal information: We do not sell or broker personal data.

6. Teen Audience & Child Privacy (Ages 13–17)

A. Intended Audience

The intended audience for Gravity Blast is teenagers aged 13 to 17 (Teenagers).

B. Treatment for Under-Age and Teen Users

  • Centralized Age-Restricted Treatment: In accordance with Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK specifications, the application configures advertising requests with AgeRestrictedTreatment.TEEN.
  • Children Under 13: Gravity Blast is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child under 13 has provided personal information through our third-party advertising partners, please contact us immediately at the contact email below, and we will take appropriate steps to assist.
  • Notice on Consent: The Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides user consent mechanisms for regional privacy frameworks but does not substitute for verified parental consent where mandatory by local law. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise their teenagers' device settings and advertising choices.
Parental Guidance Notice: Gravity Blast is intended exclusively for teenagers aged 13–17. Parents and legal guardians are encouraged to review device privacy settings and monitor digital content consumption.

7. Data Security and Transmission

  • Local Storage Security: All game preferences and statistics are stored in application-sandboxed private storage on your device (/data/data/com.gravityblaststudio.gravityblast/files/datastore/). Other standard apps on your device cannot access this storage.
  • Encryption in Transit: When advertising requests or consent synchronization occurs between your device and Google's servers, the communication is conducted over encrypted connections using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) as implemented by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Services.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

A. Local Game Data

Because your game data resides entirely on your device, you have full and direct control over its retention:

  • Clear App Data: You can delete all game progress, statistics, unlocks, and settings at any time by going to Android Settings > Apps > Gravity Blast > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstall App: Uninstalling Gravity Blast from your Android device permanently erases all locally stored game files and DataStore preferences.
  • In-Game Reset: You can reset all gameplay statistics, streaks, and scores directly inside the game by navigating to the Stats tab and tapping RESET ALL STATS.

B. Advertising Data Held by Third Parties

Google retains advertising log and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's data retention policies. Because we do not run user accounts or maintain server databases linking you to your device, we cannot directly search, view, or delete advertising telemetry collected by Google on its servers. You can manage or delete your advertising identifier through your device's Google Play Services settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted within the application and the "Last Updated" date at the top will be updated accordingly. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
